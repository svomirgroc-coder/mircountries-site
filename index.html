MirCountries Publisher — about this app

MirCountries Publisher is the internal publishing app of the MirCountries project. MirCountries creates short cinematic films about the world's most important landmarks, sacred sites and cultural heritage (exterior drone flight, interior tour and narration in several languages).

What the app does: after our automation (n8n Cloud) produces a video, MirCountries Publisher uploads it to our own YouTube channel @MirCountries using the YouTube Data API (permission "youtube.upload" only), together with the title, description, language and privacy setting. The app also publishes the same videos to our own TikTok account @mircountries.

Who uses it: only the owner of the MirCountries channels. The app is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not access, read or store any data of other people.

Privacy Policy: www.mircountries.com/privacy-policy · Terms of Service: www.mircountries.com/terms-of-service · Contact: mircountries@gmail.com
